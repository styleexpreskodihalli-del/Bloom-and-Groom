About Bloom and Groom

Bloom and Groom is a premium pet wellness brand based in Bangalore, India. We create natural, chemical-free grooming and food products that help your pets bloom with health.

Founded in 2024, our mission is to provide safe, organic, and lovingly crafted products for dogs and cats. All products are cruelty-free, vet-approved, and made with natural ingredients.
