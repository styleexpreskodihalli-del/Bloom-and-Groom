Refund & Return Policy

Last Updated: Aug 2026

At Bloom and Groom, customer satisfaction is our priority.

  • Damaged Product: If product is damaged in transit, report within 24 hours with photo on WhatsApp. Full refund or replacement.
  • Wrong Product: 100% replacement within 7 days.
  • Opened/Used: Due to hygiene, we cannot accept returns of opened food/grooming products unless manufacturing defect.
  • Refund Timeline: Refund processed within 5-7 working days to original payment method.
  • To Request: Email support@bloomandgroom.in with Order ID.

No refund for change of mind after delivery.
